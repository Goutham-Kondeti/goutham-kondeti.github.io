Healthcare, RWE & Predictive Analytics

A portfolio of SAS, Excel, SPSS and SQL work involving SAP-guided real-world evidence analysis, cohort development, statistical testing, predictive modeling and communication of healthcare findings.

Overview

Applying SAS, statistics and predictive methods to healthcare and real-world data.

This collection demonstrates practical healthcare-data analysis using SAS, Excel, SPSS and SQL. It includes a focused real-world evidence exercise in which I reviewed a Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP), mock table shells and analysis-dataset specifications, then translated those requirements into cohort logic, analysis-ready datasets and planned statistical outputs.

Selected analyses

From raw data and analysis requirements to interpretable findings.

SAS-based RWE analysis

Reviewed the M901 Statistical Analysis Plan, mock shells and analysis-dataset specifications; imported de-identified EHR domains; standardized patient, encounter and condition data; applied cohort eligibility logic; created analysis datasets; and prepared outputs for demographics, healthcare resource utilization, treatment patterns, clinical outcomes, vital signs and overall-survival analysis.

Healthcare-associated infections

Used filters, pivot tables and pivot charts to summarize more than 1,300 records and compare infection measures across selected states.

Smoking prevalence trends

Compared 1995 and 2010 smoking categories using descriptive statistics, histograms and column charts to communicate population-level changes.

Income and heart disease

Applied a chi-square test to evaluate whether income category and heart-disease status were statistically associated.

Heart-disease prediction

Prepared data in SPSS, split training and testing samples, and evaluated logistic regression and nearest-neighbor classification. Logistic regression achieved approximately 87% classification accuracy in the course exercise.

Hospital-stay queries

Used Microsoft Access SQL to retrieve institution, age, discharge and diagnostic information and calculate counts, averages and DRG-level summaries.

Clinical coding

Applied ICD-10 classification concepts to standardize diagnoses for analysis, billing and healthcare-data interpretation.

SAS and RWE workflow

Translating an SAP into reproducible analysis steps.

The exercise used a structured flow from raw EHR-style domains to standardized SAS datasets and analysis-ready cohorts. The cohort logic connected diagnosis records with encounter history and other clinical domains according to the SAP rather than relying on ad hoc filtering.

How this is presented: This is a completed SAS/RWE analysis exercise and evidence of practical SAP interpretation. It is not represented as multiple years of professional HEOR or biostatistical programming experience.

Communication

Analysis is useful only when the audience can act on it.

The coursework also evaluated chart design and communication strategy. For executives and decision-makers, the emphasis was on concise findings, appropriately selected visuals, plain language and a clear distinction between statistical association, prediction and causation.

Example: A statistically significant relationship between two variables does not prove that one caused the other. The portfolio communicates the result, assumptions and limitations together.
